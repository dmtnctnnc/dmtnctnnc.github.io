Global Money - Privacy Policy

Version 3.0 - Draft

1. Financial Data

Global Money is designed to store financial records locally on your device. These records may include income, expenses, wallets, budgets, savings goals, debts and cryptocurrency portfolios.

2. Market Data

Cryptocurrency market prices are obtained from CoinGecko. Market data requests may include cryptocurrency identifiers and standard network information.

3. Advertising

The free version is intended to use Google AdMob. Google may process device information and advertising data according to applicable privacy requirements and user consent.

Google Privacy Policy

4. Premium Purchases

Premium purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. Global Money does not directly collect payment card details for these purchases.

5. Backup and Restore

Users can export and restore supported financial records. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the application and should be stored securely.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete supported locally stored financial records through the application settings. Previously exported backup files must be deleted separately by the user.

7. Data Security

Users should protect their devices and exported financial records against unauthorized access.
